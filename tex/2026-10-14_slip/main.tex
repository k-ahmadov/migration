My goal here is to solve for fault slip due to a pressure distribution $p(x,t)$
at a given time.

The fault obeys a Mohr-Coulomb friction law with a local shear strength
$$
	\tau(x,t) = f[\sigma_{0} - p(x,t)].
$$
The shear stress drop $\Delta \tau(x,t)$ equals the quasi-static changes due to a distribution of slip $\delta$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:quasi-static}
	\Delta \tau(x,t) = \frac{G'}{\pi} \int_{-a}^{a} \frac{\partial_{s}
		\delta(s,t)}{s - x}   \, ds
\end{equation}
Where $G'=G/[2(1-\nu)]$ is in-plane (mode II) effective shear modulus and $a$ is
the crack-tip position.

The parameters of the problem can be scaled as
$$
	\frac{x}{a} \to x, \quad  \frac{p - p_{0}}{\Delta p} \to p,\quad \frac{\tau -
		\tau_{0}}{f \Delta p} \to \tau, \quad \frac{\delta G}{f \Delta p a} \to \delta
$$
The scaling non-dimensionalizes Eq. \ref{eq:quasi-static} into
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dimless-quasi-static}
	T - p(ax) = - \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-1}^{1} \frac{\partial_{s} \delta(s, t)}{x - s}
	\, ds
\end{equation}
where $T$ is the stress injection parameter
$$
	T = \frac{1 - \tau/f \sigma'_{0}}{\Delta p / \sigma'_{0}}
$$
